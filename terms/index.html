Information only

Colorado What's Up publishes general information about Colorado events, seasons and places. We work to keep it accurate, but event dates, venues, ticketing, road status, park rules and fire restrictions change often and sometimes without notice. Always confirm details with the event organizer or the managing agency before you travel. Nothing on this site is professional, legal, medical or safety advice.

Outdoor and altitude risks

Travel in Colorado's mountains involves real risks, including altitude illness, lightning, sudden weather changes, avalanches, wildfire, wildlife encounters and hazardous roads. You are responsible for your own decisions, preparation and safety. Follow the guidance of the National Park Service, Colorado Parks and Wildlife, the U.S. Forest Service, the Colorado Avalanche Information Center and local authorities, and call 911 in an emergency.
